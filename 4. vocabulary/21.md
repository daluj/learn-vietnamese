# Vocabulary A list of 50 Vietnamese words borrowed from French

Embarking on the linguistic journey of Vietnamese, you’ll soon be enchanted by the delightful presence of French words that have gracefully found a home in the language. This delightful fusion has its roots in the historical embrace of French influence during the colonial era in Vietnam. Our exploration will guide us through this charming blend, revealing how these borrowed French words have not only endured but thrived in the everyday conversations of the Vietnamese people.

-> Join us on this adventure as we uncover the unique charm and professional finesse that these French-infused expressions bring to the rich tapestry of the Vietnamese language.
