## B.1 Nước mắm (Fishsauce):

### Fish sauce is a common dipping sauce in Asian cuisine, particularly in Vietnam. However, few people are aware that fish sauce comes in a variety of varieties, which are usually classified based on the recipe used to create this well-known sauce.

![Pb5WS1X](/assets/img/vocabulary/9/Pb5WS1X.png "Danh từ chỉ Đồ vật: Món ăn (Processed Food) 55")

### *B.1a Nước mắm Thái (Fishsauce Thai style):*

### –   NƯỚC MẮM THÁI (Thai fish sauce), which is inspired by Thai cuisine, is a step up from regular chili garlic fish sauce and has a sour lemon flavor. As a result, Thai fish sauce is extremely hot and is frequently used as a dipping sauce for THỊT TÁT (Raw meats) and  HẢI SẢN (Seafood)

![6oFuJnw](/assets/img/vocabulary/9/6oFuJnw.png "Danh từ chỉ Đồ vật: Món ăn (Processed Food) 56")

### 

### -In addition, We will also learn about NƯỚC MẮM TỎI ỚT –  Chilli Garlic Fish sauce, which is used as a dipping sauce for a variety of foods (Please find some examples of dishes below)

### NƯỚC MẮM TỎI ỚT (Chilli Garlic fish sauce) is frequently served with MÓN CHIÊN- XÀO  (Fried or Stir Fry Dishes)

![Df6QvCl](/assets/img/vocabulary/9/Df6QvCl.png "Danh từ chỉ Đồ vật: Món ăn (Processed Food) 57")

![6OIjOjr](/assets/img/vocabulary/9/6OIjOjr.png "Danh từ chỉ Đồ vật: Món ăn (Processed Food) 58")

### B.1b. Nước mắm Sả (Fish sauce with Lemongrass):

### ỐC (Snail) or MÓN HẢI SẢN (Seafood meals) frequently go well with NƯỚC MẮM SẢ (Lemongrass fish sauce)

### Kumquat is a popular component in NƯỚC MẮM SẢ (Lemongrass fish sauce). Therefore, sometimes Vietnamese people call this sauce as NƯỚC MẮM SẢ TẮC (TẮC means Kumquat).

![](/assets/img/vocabulary/9/OxoaYG1.png "Danh từ chỉ Đồ vật: Món ăn (Processed Food) 59")
