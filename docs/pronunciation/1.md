## **I/y**  :

![i 2](/assets/img/chapter1/1/i-2.png "Vietnamese Alphabet 4")

### **I** and **Y** sounds similar – **“ee”** in English.

### Example (in Vietnamese word): l**y**– *glass*

### 

## **Ê:**

![ê](/assets/img/chapter1/1/ê.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 5")

### **Ê** sounds like **“eh”** in English

### Example (in Vietnamese) : l**ê** – *pear*

## **E:**

![em](/assets/img/chapter1/1/em.png "Vietnamese Alphabet 6")

### **E** sounds similar with **“air”** but **soften “r”**

### Example: m**ẹ** – *mother*

* ### **The second column:** the position of tongue is in between and opening up from **ư to a**

### (*Find picture below for each letter**)*

## **Ư**

![ư](/assets/img/chapter1/1/ư.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 7")

### **Ư** sounds similar with **“er”** but **shorten sound**

### Example: t**ừ** t**ừ** – *slow down*

## **Ơ**

![ơ](/assets/img/chapter1/1/ơ.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 8")

### **Ơ** sounds similar with **“er”** but **soften “r”**

### Example: ch**ợ** – *market*

### 

## **A**

![a](/assets/img/chapter1/1/a.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 9")

### **A** sounds like **“ah”** in English

### Example (in Vietnamese): B**a** – *three/ father (in Souther Vietnamese)*

### 

### *•* **The third column:** is the only rounded one, the position of tongue is back of throat and opening up from **u to o**

## **U**

![u](/assets/img/chapter1/1/u.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 10")

### **U** sounds like **“oo”** in English

### Example (in Vietnamese): ch**ú –** *uncle*

## 

## **Ô**

![ô](/assets/img/chapter1/1/ô.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 11")

### **Ô** sounds like **“oh”** in English

### Example (in Vietnamese): C**ô** – Aunt/Miss (Formal pronoun)

## 

## **O**

![o](/assets/img/chapter1/1/o.jpg "Vietnamese Alphabet 12")

### **O** sounds like **“aw”** in English

### Example (in Vietnamese): c**ó** –  *(to) have*
