English below:

Vietnamese culture is rich and diverse, but here are some of the most important and common cultural traits of Vietnamese people:

* Family traditions: Family plays an important role in Vietnamese life. They often maintain close relationships with their loved ones and uphold traditional family values.
* Respect for elders: Vietnamese people value respect for elders. Younger people are expected to show respect and defer to the opinions of older people and those with experience. (This is why Vietnamese people often ask the age of their counterparts to know how to address them and behave appropriately.)
* Dutifulness and filial piety: The concept of “filial piety” is important in Vietnamese culture. Children are expected to take care of and respect their parents and loved ones when they are elderly. (This explains why parents often want their children to get married and have children at a certain age so that they will not be lonely when they are old.)
* Traditional festivals: Vietnamese people often participate in many traditional festivals such as Tết Nguyên Đán (Lunar New Year), Tết Đoan Ngọ (Mid-Autumn Festival), Tết Trung Thu (Mid-Autumn Festival), and other folk festivals to commemorate and honor their ancestors.
* Religion: The majority of Vietnamese people follow religions such as Buddhism, Catholicism, Hòa Hảo, and Cao Đài. Religion plays an important role in their lives and culture.
* Arts and folk culture: Vietnamese people have many traditional art performances such as quan họ singing, chèo opera, and water puppetry. These art forms often depict stories about history and folk culture.
* Cuisine: Vietnamese cuisine is famous for dishes such as pho, bánh mì, bún chả, and many other delicacies. The dishes are often cooked and prepared fresh, with a distinctive flavor.
* Language: The Vietnamese language plays an important role in Vietnamese culture. Vietnamese people value the use of correct language and respect their mother tongue.
* Traditional dress: Áo dài is the traditional dress of Vietnamese people, often worn on special occasions and traditional festivals.
* Compassion and generosity: Vietnamese people often have generous hearts and often participate in charitable activities and help others in the community.
